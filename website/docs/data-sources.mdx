# Data Sources

Aegis ingests data from approximately 25 sources across seven categories. Each source feeds into one or more of the three researcher populations (Translational, Drug Discovery, Clinician) and is refreshed on a defined cadence. The tables below list every source, what it provides, how often it is refreshed, and which populations it serves.

## Publications

| Source | What It Provides | Refresh Cadence | Population(s) |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| PubMed | Indexed biomedical publications with MeSH descriptors, author affiliations, article types, and publication dates | Weekly | Translational, Clinician |
| bioRxiv | Preprint publications in biology, providing early signal on active research before peer review | Daily | Translational |
| medRxiv | Preprint publications in health sciences and clinical research | Daily | Translational, Clinician |
| iCite | NIH citation metrics including Relative Citation Ratio (RCR), which normalizes citation counts by field and time | Weekly | Translational |

## Grants

| Source | What It Provides | Refresh Cadence | Population(s) |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| NIH RePORTER | US federal research grants with principal investigators, activity codes, funding amounts, and active/inactive status | Weekly | Translational |
| ERC (European Research Council) | European research grants for frontier research across all disciplines | Weekly | Translational |
| Horizon Europe | EU framework programme grants covering collaborative and individual research funding | Weekly | Translational |
| MRC (Medical Research Council) | UK medical research grants with investigator details and project descriptions | Weekly | Translational |
| CIHR (Canadian Institutes of Health Research) | Canadian health research grants with investigator and funding data | Weekly | Translational |
| JST/KAKEN | Japanese research grants from the Japan Science and Technology Agency and KAKEN database, with Japanese name transliteration support | Weekly | Translational |
| NSFC (National Natural Science Foundation of China) | Chinese research grants with investigator and project data; records include a coverage caveat for transparency | Weekly | Translational |

## Patents

| Source | What It Provides | Refresh Cadence | Population(s) |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| USPTO PatentsView | US patent data with inventor names, CPC classifications, filing and grant dates, and assignee information | Weekly | Drug Discovery |
| EPO Espacenet | European patent data covering all 39 EPO member states with inventor and classification details | Weekly | Drug Discovery |
| WIPO PCT | International patent applications filed under the Patent Cooperation Treaty, providing global patent coverage | Weekly | Drug Discovery |

## Clinical

| Source | What It Provides | Refresh Cadence | Population(s) |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| ClinicalTrials.gov | Clinical trial registrations with investigator roles, trial phase, sponsor, and study design | Weekly | Translational, Clinician |
| CMS Medicare Utilization | Medicare procedure volumes by provider, used as a proxy for clinical activity and procedure expertise | Weekly | Clinician |

## Clinician Credentials

| Source | What It Provides | Refresh Cadence | Population(s) |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| NPPES/NPI | National Provider Identifier registry with provider names, specialties, practice locations, and license information | Weekly | Clinician |
| ABMS | American Board of Medical Specialties board certification status and specialty designations | Weekly | Clinician |
| State Medical Boards (10 states) | Active medical license verification and disciplinary action history across ten US states | Weekly | Clinician |
| USNWR Hospital Tier | US News and World Report hospital rankings, used to assign a hospital-tier score to affiliated clinicians | Weekly | Clinician |

## Integrity

| Source | What It Provides | Refresh Cadence | Population(s) |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Retraction Watch | Database of retracted publications, used to identify retracted co-authorships and flag potential research integrity concerns | Event-driven (&lt;6h) | All |
| ORI (Office of Research Integrity) | Federal findings of research misconduct, used as a hard-gate signal for candidate exclusion | Event-driven (&lt;6h) | All |
| OFAC/SAM | US Treasury sanctions lists (OFAC) and federal exclusion lists (SAM), used as hard-gate signals | Event-driven (&lt;6h) | All |
| LEIE (List of Excluded Individuals/Entities) | HHS exclusion list for individuals barred from federal healthcare programs | Event-driven (&lt;6h) | All |

## Taxonomy and Classification

| Source | What It Provides | Refresh Cadence | Population(s) |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| ChEMBL | Chemical biology database providing compound-target-assay relationships for mapping drug-discovery activity | Weekly | Drug Discovery |
| ICD-10/CPT-MeSH | Medical coding crosswalks that map clinical procedure codes and diagnosis codes to MeSH descriptors for specialty classification | Weekly | Clinician |
| CPC-MeSH cross-walks | Patent classification (CPC) to MeSH descriptor mappings, enabling topical-fit scoring for patent-based evidence | Weekly | Drug Discovery |

## Geographic Coverage

Aegis currently draws approximately 15% of its candidate data from non-US sources. Phase 2 established the initial international source clients (EPO, ERC, MRC, CIHR, JST/KAKEN, NSFC), and Phase 3 broadened geographic coverage with expanded ROR-normalized affiliations across JP, CN, GB, CA, EU, and AU institutions. The system includes per-region coverage diagnostics with a 75% bias threshold that triggers caveats in API responses when geographic representation is skewed.

The target is 40%+ non-US candidate coverage after Phase 3 geographic broadening is fully deployed, with ongoing tracking of per-region ratios and regression alerting to ensure coverage does not regress.
